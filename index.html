About

I started as a software engineer, building Android apps at Samsung R&D, Neemu and Accedo. Working next to designers made me want to solve the user problem, not only the technical one.

I made that move official with an M.Sc. from KTH Royal Institute of Technology (2022), where my thesis explored, through co-design, how people with ADHD and dyslexia experience streaming platforms.

At Viaplay I worked as a UX researcher and product designer on the player, personalised content blocks and the design system. Because I've written the code, I can talk trade-offs with engineers early and hand off designs that are realistic to build.
